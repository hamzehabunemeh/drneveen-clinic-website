Your Privacy

Your trust, protected

How we collect, use and protect the information you share with us.

P

Who we are

This website belongs to Clinica Neveen — the clinic of Dr. Neveen Dababneh for dermatology, aesthetics and laser therapy, located at the 7th Circle, Amman, Jordan. For any privacy question, contact us at emadnivo@gmail.com.

Information we collect

When you submit the booking form, we collect the details you provide: your name, phone number, email (optional), the treatment you are interested in, your preferred date and time, and any notes you choose to share.

We do not ask for sensitive medical records through this website. Please do not send detailed medical information through the form — we will discuss it with you privately during your consultation.

How we use it

  • To contact you and confirm your appointment.
  • To prepare for your visit and answer your questions.
  • To send a reminder about your appointment, where needed.

We never sell or rent your personal information.

Third-party services

Booking requests are delivered to the clinic by email through FormSubmit. The site also uses Google Maps to show our location and Google Fonts for typography; these services may process technical data such as your IP address according to their own policies.

Photos & patient consent

Before & after photos are published only with the written consent of our patients. Patients may withdraw their consent at any time and the photos will be removed.

Your rights

You may ask us at any time to access, correct or delete the information you have sent us. Contact us by email or WhatsApp and we will respond promptly.

Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.
