About the Doctor

Dr. Neveen Dababneh

Dr. Neveen Dababneh is a dermatologist dedicated to medical and aesthetic skin care. Her approach is simple: listen first, diagnose precisely, and treat with restraint — enhancing each patient's natural features rather than changing them.

From acne and hair loss to laser therapy and facial contouring, every plan she creates is built on medical knowledge, honest advice and a genuine care for how you feel in your own skin.
